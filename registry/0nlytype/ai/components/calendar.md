# 0nlyType: calendar

Extracted from DESIGN.md.

### db-month (calendar)
- Underneath: hook (a grid with a roving tab stop).
- From the "28 December" poster: a huge numeral, the month over the year beside it and the weekday at the far side, then a grid of large discs, one per day. The grid of discs is the picture: time spent and time left, at a glance.
- Anatomy: `.db-month[data-variant="dots | ruler | ghost | parenthesis"]` holding `.db-month-head` (`.db-month-at` with the numeral `.db-month-date` and `.db-month-dayname`, the `.db-month-title` month in caps over the year, `.db-month-nav` with two hairline chevrons), then `.db-month-grid` with 7 `<abbr>` weekday heads and weeks of `<button role="gridcell" class="db-month-day" data-when="past | today | future" aria-selected>` each holding `.db-month-disc` > `.db-month-n`, today with `aria-current="date"`, then `.db-month-foot`. Before the 1st there is only paper (`.db-month-gap`); after the last day, nothing: the neighbouring months never show.
- dots (default): gone is a solid ink disc, today the one accent disc, to come a hairline ring (`--db-rule-strong`). The number is reversed out of the ink it sits on, like "the uncreative": paper on ink (over 15:1 in day and nocturne), paper on the accent (the accent clears 4.5:1 on paper in every scheme and key, so paper on it does too), and pencil inside a ring so the days to come stay back. `--f` is how full of ink a disc is, from the bottom.
- dots, the wait: the days between today and the chosen day are half-moons (`data-between`), after the eclipse poster: ink under a horizon, the number in the open sky above it, no ring. As in the poster, the disc is narrower than its horizon: the moon is about half its day's width, and the horizon is one `--db-rule-strong` hairline that runs on through the gaps from today to yours, so the wait reads as one span and stays lighter than the days gone. Once you choose (or turn the month), they wax in turn toward your day (`--k` × `--db-arpeggio`).
- dots, yours: the chosen day is circled, like a date on a paper calendar: an ink ring at stroke width outside its disc, its number in the italic, larger. It adds no second accent; chosen today, the accent disc is circled. Pointing sketches the same ring in pencil, drawn round from the top (`--sweep`, a conic mask); choosing inks it and it lands with spiccato.
- ruler: the month as one measured line, after Paul Rand's dimension lines and SPECTRA's huge type against tiny data. A tick a day (`.db-month-disc` restyled) on a baseline that is ink as far as today; the weeks' first days and the 1st stand taller and carry their number (every day is numbered once the line is 40rem long; a label gives way to a neighbouring today or yours). Today is the accent tick with a dot at its head, yours a full ink tick with a ring at its head and its number in italic. The numeral stands over its tick, clamped inside the line, with the weekday beside it on the side facing the middle. Under the line, `.db-month-span` measures the wait as a dimension from today to your day with the count under its middle, open at an end that runs off the month. The rows become `display: contents`, so the grid is one row of `--days` columns; the weekday heads stay for screen readers only.
- ghost: after "the silence that heals", numbers as data against huge ghost numerals. The days are figures only, no discs: gone in pencil, to come in ink, today the accent figure (700), yours in the italic. Behind them `.db-month-at` becomes the ghost: the chosen day (else today) stands over its month's number (`.db-month-mo`, "24" over "09"), light (200), at 58cqi of the month's own width so it holds at popover size, in half the rule's tone, cropped by the month's end and foot edges (`overflow: clip`, `container-type: inline-size`). The grid is read through it. It turns italic with yours, at four fifths of the size. The head is the month over the year with the chevrons at the far side; the weekday name is left to the column heads. Pointing sketches a pencil hairline under the figure, drawn out from its middle. Forced colours drop the ghost; the italic still marks yours.
- parenthesis: after "20(25)", the typographic parenthetical. The month is set as one running line of figures at `mp` that wraps like a sentence (the rows become `display: contents`; the weekday heads stay for screen readers only), a wider space (0.6em) closing each week. The time from today to your day, whichever comes first, is an parenthesis: `data-paren="open"` hangs a "(" before the first of the two and `data-paren="close"` a ")" after the other, both light (200) and a little taller than the figures, hung in the space between figures (absolutely placed, like hanging punctuation) so nothing reflows. The figures inside are set heavier (650). An aside that began last month only closes here. Gone is pencil, to come is ink, today the accent, yours the italic. Pointing sketches pencil parentheses round a figure; choosing inks the aside and its parentheses land with spiccato. Right to left, the parentheses mirror with the text (bidi mirroring).
- Focus in ghost and parenthesis is a ring, not a box: the day is rounded (`border-radius: 50%`) so the outline draws an ellipse round the figure; parenthesis draws it with no offset.
- ruler, the hand: the ticks are narrow, so pressing and dragging along the line sketches the day under the pointer in pencil, and letting go chooses it (`data-scrub`, pointer capture, `touch-action: pan-y`). A key's click still chooses.
- Yours, in every variant: the big numeral turns to the chosen day in the italic, and the weekday follows it. dots and parenthesis roll it; ruler glides it along the line; ghost sets a new ghost that surfaces.
- The foot: one tracked line in caps that says something true: "12 days to go", "Tomorrow", "Today is the day", "The last day of September", "9 days left in September". English only; `caption` replaces it or, as null, removes it.
- Turning the month, the days sweep in, in reading order, from the side you turned toward (`data-turn="next | prev"` on the grid, `--d` = the cell's place), mirrored right to left.
- States: rest, gone / today / the wait / yours / to come, chosen today, hover, focus (on the day that holds the tab stop), disabled days, and for ruler the wait run on from another month; ghost with a chosen day, with none (today behind) and pointed; parenthesis enclosing the wait, run on from another month, and pointed. The keyboard is the same in every variant, the running line included.
- Keyboard: roving tabindex; arrow keys move by a day (following the direction) or a week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Home and End to the ends of the week; Enter or Space chooses. Each day is named in full: "Wednesday 30 September 2026, today".

## Motion

| Component | Articulation | What moves |
|---|---|---|
| Month (`calendar`) | Arpeggio | A pencil ring draws round the day you point at and inks when you choose, landing with spiccato; the moons wax in turn; the days sweep in from the turn |
| Month, ruler (`calendar`) | Legato | The numeral glides along the line to your day and the dimension stretches after it; dragging sketches the day under the hand in pencil |
| Month, ghost (`calendar`) | Surfacing | A pencil hairline draws out under the figure you point at; choosing (or turning the month) sets a new ghost that rises into view, the month's number an arpeggio behind the day's |
| Month, parenthesis (`calendar`) | Spiccato | Pointing sketches pencil parentheses round a figure; choosing inks the aside and its parentheses land with one small rebound |
